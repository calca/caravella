## 2.5.1 (24.11.2026)

- **Changed**: Group cards and the group page are calmer: one quiet line under the title with favorite, sharing state and type, and the top bar takes the group's color as you scroll
- **Fixed**: Sync over Wi-Fi no longer stalls when the other phone stops answering, and "Update now" no longer cuts off an exchange in progress
- **Fixed**: Sync history shows each exchange once, with what was really sent and received
- **Fixed**: Pairing works only while the group's invitation is open, and a phone that forgot yours asks you to pair again
- **Fixed**: The status bar stays readable over a bright group photo in dark theme

## 2.5.0 (23.11.2026)

- **New**: Group budget: in total or per month, week or day. The budget sheet shows what's left, how much a day keeps you within it and when it would run out at this pace
- **New**: A second currency per group for trips abroad: type expenses in the local currency, with the rate from your card statement; balances stay in the group's currency
- **New**: Settle up: tick a suggested payment as paid with one tap
- **New**: Google Drive sync between your own devices now works (Play version)
- **New**: After an update, a short notice and a dot on Settings point you to what's new
- **Improvements**: Redesigned group cards, and a clearer expense page with the description as its title
- **Improvements**: Faster: groups open at once, saving in big groups is quicker and the app starts sooner
- **Improvements**: Predictive back on Android, the same gentle motion for every sheet, better TalkBack support and large text
- **Fixes**: Sync no longer loses expenses when two phones change a group at the same time
- **Fixes**: The place picker opens on where you are, the calendar shows the whole month, and notifications and the note no longer end up under the buttons

## 2.4.0 (09.11.2026)

- **New**: Sharing a group is one flow: **Invite a device** shows the QR code and makes the phone visible to nearby phones over Bluetooth; on the other phone, **Join a group** reads the code or lists the nearby phones
- **New**: Two privacy switches in Sync with others, **Sync over Wi-Fi** and **Sync over Bluetooth**: with both off, the phone isn't visible to anyone
- **New**: Paired devices say how they're doing, and **Update now** updates over Wi-Fi or, if needed, over Bluetooth
- **New**: Settling up suggests as few payments as possible
- **Improvements**: A group with a color looks like one with a photo, and a new group shows its card as you create it
- **Improvements**: When sync has a problem, Settings › Synchronization explains what to fix; fewer messages at the bottom of the screen, and problems shown where they happen
- **Improvements**: Adding an expense is smoother: the people and categories lists open inside the sheet, and "More details" grows the sheet into the full page; the group page uses the same quick sheet as Home
- **Fixes**: The place of an expense can be changed on the map; the app starts in your phone's language; voice input no longer asks for the Nearby devices permission; the F-Droid version can scan the QR code to join a group
