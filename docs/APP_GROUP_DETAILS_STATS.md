# App: Group Details & Stats

Covers `lib/manager/details/**` — the per-group detail screen, the stats tabs, the settlements ("who owes whom") algorithm, the locations map, search, group settings, and data export.

## Group detail page

`ExpenseGroupDetailPage` (`details/pages/expense_group_detail_page.dart`) is the main per-group screen, in the Home / Groups style: `surfaceContainer` page, one `CustomScrollView` with

- **Header** — `GroupHeaderBand` (`widgets/group_detail_header.dart`) at the top of the page: a block in the group's tint from the top of the screen with rounded bottom corners (or the photo melting into its own tone; see [Home](APP_HOME.md) for `GroupLook` and `PhotoTone`), under the round buttons of `GroupDetailTopBar`, with the big type icon (`TypeMark`) below them and `GroupDetailHeader` in the tint's text color, 30 dp from the edges. Over a color the top bar's title uses `GroupHeaderBand.foregroundOf` and the status bar follows. The page is edge to edge (`AppSystemUI.edgeToEdge`: transparent system navigation bar). `GroupDetailHeader`: a heart when the group is a favorite and the group type's icon (its name for screen readers), dates · participant count, the title, and a `FigureRow` "Total | Today" (`getTodaySpendingSync`), one under the other with large text. With a budget (`ExpenseGroup.budget`) Today has a `BudgetRing` (24 dp) after its amount, on the same line (`caravella_core_ui`, from `BudgetStatus.todayFraction`: in the tint's text color with a faint track while within it, full in muted amber `budgetOver` when over today's share, never red), and tapping it opens `BudgetDetailSheet` (`lib/manager/budget/budget_detail_sheet.dart`): today (left of today's share, or how far over), the current period (left or over, `BudgetBar` with a marker for how far the period has gone, per day until the end and average so far) and, for monthly, weekly and daily budgets, the three periods before on the budget's scale (a `Table`, so the bars line up); amounts in `CurrencyDisplay`, as elsewhere; "Change budget" opens `showBudgetSettingsSheet` (`budget_settings_sheet.dart`, period and amount, "Remove budget"), saved with `ExpenseGroupStorageV2.updateGroupBudget`. The same sheet is the Budget row of the group's settings (`GroupSettingsPage`, under Categories), saved at once. Without a budget Today has no ring and no tap. The per-person share is a pill in the Statistics' spending pace card.
- **Selector** — a `PillSegmentedControl` (from `caravella_core_ui`) **Expenses / Balances / Statistics** (`_section`); the selected fill slides like the floating navigation's and the new content fades in (`_sectionFade`, a `SliverFadeTransition`, skipped with `disableAnimations`). With no expenses Balances and Statistics are disabled and the page goes back to Expenses:
  - Expenses: `WeekGroupedExpenseSliver` (`widgets/grouped_expense_list.dart`) — one card per calendar week (Monday–Sunday), newest first; the header names it ("This week", "Last week", else the date range) with the week total; rows are `ExpenseDayRow`s (`widgets/expense_day_row.dart`: the day like a calendar — weekday over day number —, title, "payer · category", amount; 12 dp above and below) opening the edit sheet; a just-added expense fades in (`highlightedExpenseId`). With no expenses, `EmptyExpenseState`.
  - Balances: `GroupBalancesView` (`widgets/group_balances_view.dart`) — first a "Share per person" card (`_FairShareCard`: "split equally among N participants" — the total is already in the header —, the fair share `computeBalances` measures against; amount under the title with large text), then a bar per participant (with "Paid X in all" under the name, from `getParticipantTotals`, plus "· Y paid back" / "· Y received" from the ticked Settle up payments, so the balance reads against the share per person above) from `outstandingBalances` (the Settle up payments still to make, summed per person in whole cents, so the bars match Settle up to the cent and all read zero once everything is ticked; `computeBalances` would show fractions of a cent when the total doesn't split evenly) in one hue (right/primary = gets back, left/lighter primary = owes; amounts primary / onSurface), then "Settle up": the `computeSettlements` payments still to make, each with a share button sending a reminder to the payer (`balance_reminder_message`) and, at the right, a clock that ticks it as paid in full (`markSettlementPaid`, no amount to type), followed by the payments already ticked (`ExpenseGroup.payments`, newest first: names struck through, "Paid · <date>", a filled check that ticks it back with `unmarkPayment`, no reminder); "All settled up" heads the card when nothing is left to pay. The ticks go to `onPaymentsChanged`, which `ExpenseGroupDetailPage._savePayments` shows at once and saves with `ExpenseGroupStorageV2.updateGroupPayments` (synced with the group); payments never appear in the expense list, totals or statistics, and "Share balances" sending the whole summary (payments, total, per person) — both through the system share sheet (`share`, injectable for tests); or "All settled up".
  - Statistics: `GroupStatisticsView` (`widgets/group_statistics_view.dart`), from the overview to the details — the long category list last: `SpendingPaceCard` first (the group's total spread over every day of its period — or every month, for groups longer than two months — from `SpendingPace.of` in `caravella_core`, with what it's averaged over and pills for the per-person share, the expense count, the average expense and how many days/months had any); one spending chart in a card titled above it, over the whole group (`buildGroupSpendingSeries` in `daily_totals_utils.dart`: from the start date or first expense to the end date or last expense — an ongoing group stops at today — day by day up to 31 days, week by week up to ~6 months, month by month beyond; titled "Day by day" / "Week by week" / "Month by month" with the period; drawn as bars from zero by `SpendingBars`, so a single day of expenses or equal amounts still show, with the amounts above the bars up to 7 of them); highest expense and most expensive day, the chart's peaks (`highestExpense`, `mostExpensiveDay` — reusing `WeekGroupedExpenseSliver.groupByDay`; `mostExpensiveMonth` instead of the day when the pace is per month); `GroupPayersBubbles` (the top `maxShown` = 4 payers, one row on a phone however big the group, each avatar sized by what they paid, area ∝ amount); `GroupMapCard` (always shown; `groupPlacesCount` counts distinct coordinates rounded to ~100 m): with places it opens `ExpenseLocationsMapPage`; without any, if the group's automatic location is off it says so and opens the group's Other settings (`ExpenseGroupOtherPage`, via `onEnableLocation`) where it's turned on, otherwise it's a non-tappable "No expenses with a place yet" note; then `CategoriesOverviewTab(embedded: true)`. With large text (scale ≥ 1.3) the record cards stack one per row (`_RecordCards`), and the chart period and the pace's unit wrap under the title / amount instead of overflowing. Total, period and participants aren't repeated (they're in the header).
- **Welcome** — a group received from another device and not opened yet shows `GroupWelcomeCard` (`widgets/group_welcome_card.dart`) above the section selector until OK or Add expense (see [Sync Architecture](SYNC_ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- **Top bar** — `GroupDetailTopBar` (`details/widgets/group_detail_top_bar.dart`), an overlay with round buttons (back, search, ⋯) that turns opaque as soon as content scrolls under it (`_barOpaque`) and shows the group name once the title scrolls away (`_collapsedTitleVisible`). The ⋯ menu (`GroupMenuItem`s in a `MenuAnchor` whose items are at least 248 dp wide so its end lines up with the button) holds favorite/unfavorite, synchronization and settings (`GroupSettingsPage`); the map opens from its card in Statistics, and share & export (`showExpenseExportOptionsSheet`) is in the group's settings only. In the header, a synced group's "Shared" pill (`GroupSyncBadge` with `onTap` — a 48 dp touch target around the small pill —, its dot showing the sync state — see [Sync Architecture](SYNC_ARCHITECTURE.md)) also opens the sync page; on Home's card it is the same pill without the chevron.
- A round, hide-on-scroll `AddFab`, on Expenses only (on Balances it would cover the payments' paid buttons; it slides away when switching section) and the expense add/edit sheet (`lib/manager/expense/pages/expense_form_page.dart` — see [Expense Entry](APP_EXPENSE_ENTRY.md)).

The old separate Overview page (`UnifiedOverviewPage` with General / Participants / Categories tabs) is gone: its content lives in Statistics and Balances.

## Stats helpers

`CategoriesOverviewTab` (`pages/tabs/categories_overview_tab.dart`) renders one `StatCard` per category (total, per day — or per month — over the same period as `SpendingPace`, % bar); the group page uses it with `embedded: true` (no own scroll view or padding).

Chart/series helpers live in `pages/tabs/usecase/`:

- `daily_totals_utils.dart` — `calculateDailyTotalsOptimized(group, startDate, days)` buckets expense amounts into a fixed-length daily array; `buildWeeklySeries`/`buildMonthlySeries` wrap it ("this week starting Monday" / "this calendar month"); `buildAdaptiveDateRangeSeries` picks the group's explicit date range if ≤30 days, else falls back to the actual expense date span (also capped at 30 days); `shouldShowDateRangeChart` decides whether `GroupStatisticsView` shows one combined date-range chart instead of separate weekly+monthly charts.
- `date_range_formatter.dart` — `formatDateRange({start, end, locale})`, omitting the year when it matches the current year, always showing both years when a range crosses a year boundary.

> `settlements_logic.dart` is just a re-export shim (`export 'package:caravella_core/caravella_core.dart' show Settlement, computeSettlements;`) — the real algorithm is in `caravella_core`; the stats logic lives on `ExpenseGroup` (`getTotalExpenses`, `getCategoryTotals`, …) and in `SpendingPace` (period, unit and averages: the period runs from the start date or first expense to the end date when it's already past; a group without one, or still running, counts up to today while it's active — an expense in the last `activeWithinDays` (14) days — and up to its last expense otherwise, so a weekend from months ago stays a few days, not a long period per month; always covering every expense).

## Settlements algorithm

The "who owes whom" logic is `computeSettlements(ExpenseGroup trip)` in `packages/caravella_core/lib/model/group_settlements.dart` (re-exported through the shim above for convenience):

1. Returns `[]` if fewer than 2 participants or no expenses.
2. Balances are **exact integers**: amounts in cents, each participant's balance as `paid·n − total` (cents × number of people), so an equal share that doesn't divide evenly (100 € among 3) still adds up to zero — an **equal-split assumption**; there is no per-expense weighting or itemized-share support. `computeBalances(trip)` (used directly by `GroupBalancesView`) is the same divided back to currency units. Positive = creditor (is owed money), negative = debtor. Payments ticked as paid (`ExpenseGroup.payments`, `SettlePayment`: from, to, amount, date) then move the two people's balances by `amount` (in the same scaled cents); one naming someone no longer in the group is ignored. They aren't spending, so the total and the share per person don't change.
3. **Fewest payments**: with k people holding a balance, k − 1 payments always suffice, and each set of them whose balances add up to zero on their own saves one (they settle among themselves). So the minimum is k minus the largest number of such sets; a DP over the 2^k subsets finds it (`exactSettlementLimit` = 16 people with a balance, ~1–2 ms; above that everyone is one set). A set may be off by up to a cent — that cent is left out instead of becoming a "0.01" payment. Among the splits with the fewest payments it keeps the one whose smallest payment is largest.
4. Each set gets **whole-cent** balances adding up to zero (rounded down, then the missing cents to whoever lost most in rounding — on a tie to whoever it brings closer to zero, so half a cent isn't owed), then within the set the largest debt pays the largest credit until everyone is even. Results are `Settlement(fromId, toId, amount)` (participant **IDs**, not names — survives renames), largest amount first.

Example: balances +4, +3, +3, −6, −4 — plain largest-to-largest needs 4 payments, this finds 3 (−4 → +4; −6 → +3 and +3). Consumers: `GroupBalancesView` (Balances, with each payment's reminder message and the shared summary) and `MarkdownExporter.generate`, so all show the same plan. Tests: `packages/caravella_core/test/group_settlements_test.dart` (incl. random groups checked against a brute-force search).

If you ever need to support unequal/itemized splits, this is the function to change — but note it's a fundamental behavior change (currently *whoever pays* is credited the full amount, not *whoever benefits*).

## Locations map

`expense_locations_map_page.dart` filters expenses down to those with a non-null lat/lng, computes bounds via `computeBounds` (see [caravella_core_ui reference](PACKAGE_CARAVELLA_CORE_UI.md)), and renders pins on a `flutter_map` (OSM). Pins that would overlap on screen (closer than 44 logical px at the current zoom) are merged into one numbered pin; the clustering runs in a `Builder` inside the map's layers, so it's recomputed on every zoom/pan and zooming in splits a group back up. Tapping a pin opens `ExpenseMapDetailSheet`, which shows a single address + coordinates when every expense in the group shares the spot, or the distinct place names otherwise. See [Location & Maps](LOCATION_AND_MAPS.md).

## Search

`expense_search_page.dart` (`ExpenseSearchPage`) is full-text + filter search over a group's expenses (name / note / category / payer / place / amount), in the app's style (`SearchTopBar`, `FilterPill`, `PillSection`, `SearchSummaryBar` from `caravella_core_ui`). Before typing (no query, no filters) it shows the filters as suggestions: paid by, categories, period (today, last 7 days, this month, a range via `PeriodSelectionBottomSheet`), with attachment / with place. The filters button opens the same choices in a sheet ("Show N results"); active filters stay as removable pills (plus "Clear" when there are several). Results show their count and total, then one card of `ExpenseDayRow`s (`showMonth`: month over day number, since results span weeks) with the match highlighted in title and "payer · category"; an expense found only through its place or note gets a line under the row saying so (`_matchReason`).

## Group settings

`group_settings_page.dart` (`GroupSettingsPage`) uses the same layout as the app settings (`SettingsScaffold`, see [Settings](APP_SETTINGS.md#settings-hub)): eyebrow "Group", "Settings" title, a `SettingsProfileCard` with the group's photo / color / type icon, title and dates · people · currency (tap → `ExpenseGroupGeneralPage`; tapping the picture itself opens it with the background sheet already open), then **Group** (participants and categories, with their first names as subtitle; Other → `ExpenseGroupOtherPage`), **Share & export** (sync page, export options — disabled without expenses) and **Danger zone** (archive / unarchive, delete in the error color). The editor pages are described in [Group Management](APP_GROUP_MANAGEMENT.md); they use the same `SettingsScaffold` layout (General: name, period, currency and background in cards; Participants / Categories: `EditableNameList` rows in a card, `showHeader: false`; Other: notification / auto-location switches; Sync: `ExpenseGroupSyncPage`).

## Data export

All in `export/`, static classes with `generate(...)`/`buildFilename(...)`:

| Exporter | Format | Filename pattern |
|---|---|---|
| `CsvExporter` | One row per expense (name, amount, paidBy, category, ISO date, note, location text) | `YYYYMMDD_<slug-title>_export.csv` |
| `MarkdownExporter` | Full report: metadata, per-participant/per-category totals, settlements section, expense table; amounts use the decimal separator of the language | `YYYYMMDD_<slug-title>_export.md` |
| `OfxExporter` | Pseudo-OFX 2.0 XML (`<STMTTRN>` per expense, all typed `DEBIT`) for import into personal-finance apps | `YYYY-MM-DD_<slug-title>_export.ofx` |
