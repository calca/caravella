# Marketing media

- `caravella-sync.gif` (720×540, 11 s loop): sharing a group between two phones. Used at the top of the app README and in GitHub release notes.
- `caravella-sync.mp4` (1200×900): the same clip, for posts on Reddit, Mastodon and Lemmy.

The clip has four parts: the app icon with "Share a group with friends", the Invite a device sheet with its QR code, the "Norway Road Trip" group on both phones ("End-to-end encrypted. No server, no account."), and the outro with the Google Play link and duea.page/caravella. The invite frame is `store/screenshots/raw/04-qr.png` with the emulator warning and the old sync subtitle painted over, as in the published store screenshot 04. It was rendered from an HTML stage with Playwright and encoded with ffmpeg (two-pass palette).
