Caravella is the simple way to split expenses with friends, family and roommates. Create a group for a trip, a shared flat or an event, add what everyone spends, and Caravella tells you who owes whom — no spreadsheets, no arguments.

<b>A clear, calm design</b>
• Home shows your group with its total, today's spending and your latest expenses
• Create a group in a few short steps and add friends right away
• Each group has its page with expenses, balances and statistics — swipe between them
• Soft group colors or your own photo, light and dark theme

<b>Add expenses in seconds</b>
• Pick who paid and the category with a single tap
• Say it out loud: amount and description are filled in for you
• Attach a photo of the receipt, notes and the place where you spent

<b>Know who owes whom</b>
• Automatic balances and settle-up suggestions
• Spending pace, day-by-day and monthly charts
• Breakdown by participant and category
• Search groups and expenses, with filters

<b>Sync with friends (optional)</b>
• Share a group with devices on the same Wi-Fi, paired with a QR code
• End-to-end encrypted, never through a server

<b>Your data, your rules</b>
• Works offline, no account required
• Your data stays on your device
• Backup, restore and CSV export at any time
• Available in English, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese
• Open source (MIT license)
